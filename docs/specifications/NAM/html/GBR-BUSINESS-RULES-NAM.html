GBR-IDRule StatementApplies ToRationaleEnforced By
GBR-AU-01Only an account with the Active status can log in; an account that is Pending activation, Inactive or Locked is rejected.UC-02.1 (step 6)Keep accounts that are not activated or were deactivated by the Shop Owner out of the system.System (authentication service)
GBR-AU-02The system finds the account by email (ignoring letter case) and compares the password with the encrypted password; a non-existent email and a wrong password share one message; login is by email only.UC-02.1 (step 7)Protect the stored passwords and do not reveal which emails are registered.System (authentication service, password encryption)
GBR-AU-03Every successful login creates a new session, independent of other sessions; the access token is valid for 30 minutes and the refresh token and session for at most 15 days; the system returns the user information and the active roles.UC-02.1 (step 8)Limit the risk of a leaked token while keeping login convenient and allowing use on several devices.System (authentication service, token issuing, session storage)
GBR-AC-01The system has exactly 5 fixed roles: Shop Owner, Sales Staff, Accountant, Shipper, Customer. The system does not allow creating, renaming or deleting roles; roles are always shown in a fixed order.UC-03.5 (step 2)Keep the permission model simple, stable and aligned with the store's user groups.System (role and permission service) and database (fixed roles cannot be renamed or deleted)
GBR-AC-02Only the Shop Owner can view the roles and change the permissions of a role.UC-03.5 (step 1)Access rights are sensitive; only the store owner may decide them.System (user role check)
GBR-AC-03A change of role permissions takes effect on the next request of every account with that role, including the Shop Owner who made the change; users do not need to log in again.UC-03.5 (step 9)Permissions always reflect the latest configuration; no outdated permission stays in effect.System (the user's permissions are determined from current data on every request)
GBR-CG-01The category code is unique in the whole system (ignoring letter case) and cannot be changed after creation.UC-06.1 (step 7), UC-06.2 (step 2)The code is a stable key that other functions (products, Excel import) use to refer to a category.System (category service) and database (unique key)
GBR-CG-02A category name is unique under the same parent category; root categories cannot have the same name.UC-06.1 (step 7), UC-06.2 (step 5)Avoid confusion when choosing among categories on the same level.System (category service)
GBR-CG-03A category has at most one parent category, or is a root category. The parent category must exist; a category cannot be its own parent and cannot take its own child or descendant as its parent (no loops). The system does not limit the number of levels.UC-06.1 (step 8), UC-06.2 (step 5)Keep the tree valid and prevent loops that would break walking through the category tree.System (category service) and database (parent foreign key)
GBR-CG-04A category with the Inactive status is hidden from the category selection lists (choosing a parent category, choosing a category for a product), from the Excel product import and from the category list shown to customers in the store; it can still be seen in the Category Management page.UC-06.1 (step 4), UC-06.2 (step 4)Allow a category to stop being used without losing data and without affecting customers.System (the category, store and Excel import services only use active categories)
GBR-CG-05Deleting a category is a soft delete: the category changes to the Inactive status and its data is kept. A category that has child categories (even inactive ones) cannot be deleted. Deleting a category that is already Inactive causes no error and changes nothing.UC-06.2 (step 4 of the "Delete a category" flow)Keep the history and the product references, and do not orphan child categories.System (category service)
GBR-CG-06Changing a category to the Inactive status does not change the status of its child categories.UC-06.2 (step 3_Hide or show a category again)Allow hiding each category separately; the user decides whether to hide the whole branch.System (category service)
GBR-BD-01The brand code is unique in the whole system (ignoring letter case) and cannot be changed after creation.UC-06.3 (step 7, step 2 of the "Edit a brand" flow)The code is a stable key that other functions (products, warranty policies, Excel import) use to refer to a brand.System (brand service) and database (unique key)
GBR-BD-02The brand name is unique in the whole system (ignoring letter case and leading or trailing spaces).UC-06.3 (step 7, step 4 of the "Edit a brand" flow)Avoid two identical brands that cause confusion when choosing a brand for a product.System (brand service)
GBR-BD-03A brand with the Inactive status is hidden from the brand selection lists, from the Excel product import and from the brand list shown to customers in the store; it can still be seen in the Brand Management page.UC-06.3 (step 2 of the "Hide or show a brand again" flow)Allow a brand to stop being used without losing data and without affecting customers.System (the brand, store and Excel import services use active brands only)
GBR-BD-04Deleting a brand is a soft delete: the brand changes to the Inactive status and its data is kept. The system does not check whether products use the brand. Deleting a brand that is already Inactive causes no error and changes nothing.UC-06.3 (step 4 of the "Delete a brand" flow)Keep the history and the references of products and warranty policies that already use the brand.System (brand service)
GBR-PY-01A payment can be recorded only for an order that exists, is not Cancelled and has a total amount greater than 0.UC-23 (step 3, step 8)Do not collect money for an order that is no longer valid.Screen (button disabled) and the system (order check)
GBR-PY-02The recorded amount cannot exceed the remaining balance of the order. Remaining balance = order total − the sum of verified receipts − pending cash-on-delivery (COD) receipts.UC-23 (step 6, step 8)Do not collect more than the order's obligation and avoid overlapping with a pending COD receipt.Screen (checked first) and the system (final check)
GBR-PY-03Cash actually received by the staff member is recorded as "Đã xác minh" (Verified) immediately: the receipt is added to the paid amount, the order's payment status becomes Fully paid when the paid amount equals the order total (otherwise Partially paid), and the system automatically allocates the receipt to the customer's eligible electronic invoices, oldest invoice first.UC-23 (step 9)Cash has really been received, so it is reflected in the order and the customer's debt immediately.System (payment service, order status update, payment allocation)
GBR-PY-04A bank transfer declared by the customer is recorded as "Chờ xác minh" (Pending); it is not counted in the paid amount and does not change the payment status of the order until an accountant verifies or rejects it in the bank transfer verification use case.UC-23 (step 9)The system cannot check the bank automatically, so an authorized person must confirm the money has arrived.System (payment service)
GBR-PY-05Only Sales Staff and Shop Owner can record payments; an Accountant can only view the history, summary and milestone progress.UC-23 (step 1)Separate receiving money from verifying money to control fraud.System (role check) and screen (button hidden for users without permission)
GBR-VF-01Only users with the Accountant role use the bank transfer verification page; users with other roles only see the message “Chỉ dành cho Kế toán”.UC-14.1 (step 1)Separate verifying money from receiving money (Sales Staff) to control fraud.Screen (page blocked) and the system (role check)
GBR-VF-02Only a bank transfer in the "Chờ xác minh" (Pending) status can be verified or rejected. A transfer that is already verified or rejected cannot be decided again.UC-14.1 (step 5, step 4 of "Reject the bank transfer")Each transfer has exactly one final decision, so money is never added twice.System (payment verification service)
GBR-VF-03The verified amount cannot exceed the order's remaining balance. Remaining balance = order total − verified receipts − pending cash-on-delivery (COD) receipts. If it is exceeded, the transfer stays "Chờ xác minh" for handling outside the system; the system does not refund or change the order status automatically.UC-14.1 (step 5)Do not record more than the order's obligation; handling the excess needs a human decision.System (payment verification service)
GBR-VF-04Verifying a bank transfer: the transfer becomes "Đã xác minh" with the verifier and time; the amount is added to the paid amount; the order's payment status becomes Fully paid when the paid amount equals the order total (otherwise Partially paid); the system allocates the receipt to the customer's eligible electronic invoices, oldest first.UC-14.1 (step 6)Only after the accountant confirms the money has arrived does the transfer count in the debt and the order.System (payment verification service, order status update, payment allocation)
GBR-VF-05Rejecting a bank transfer: the reason is required, at most 1000 characters; the transfer becomes "Đã từ chối" with the reason, the user and the time; it is not counted in the paid amount and the order's payment status does not change.UC-14.1 ("Reject the bank transfer" flow)Keep the basis of the decision for traceability and let the staff know the transfer was not accepted.Reject dialog (reason check) and the system (re-validation, storing the decision)
GBR-GR-01Goods can be received only for a purchase order that exists and is in the "Đã xác nhận" or "Nhận một phần" status.UC-16.1 (step 2, step 7)Only receive goods for orders that are approved and not yet completed, cancelled or closed.Screen (button hidden) and the system (receiving service, purchase order lock and check)
GBR-GR-02Only lines that belong to the purchase order can be received; the received quantity of each line cannot exceed the remaining quantity (ordered quantity minus received quantity).UC-16.1 (step 7)Avoid receiving more than was ordered from the supplier.System (receiving service and purchase order service)
GBR-GR-03Creating a receipt confirms it immediately: the receipt is "Đã xác nhận" and cannot be edited or cancelled; the receipt code is generated by the system; the receipt date is the creation date; the line amount equals the purchase unit cost on the purchase order times the received quantity and the receipt total equals the sum of the line amounts; the on-hand stock of the SKU in the purchase order's warehouse increases by exactly the received quantity and is recorded in the stock history.UC-16.1 (step 8, step 9)Stock changes only with a clear, traceable receiving document that cannot be changed afterwards.System (receiving, stock and stock history services)
GBR-GR-04After a receipt is created, the received quantity of each purchase order line increases by the quantity received. The purchase order becomes "Hoàn thành" when all lines have received the full ordered quantity, otherwise "Nhận một phần".UC-16.1 (step 10)The purchase order reflects the real receiving progress so that it is known what is still to be received.System (purchase order service)
GBR-IV-01The available quantity of a SKU = on-hand quantity − reserved quantity.UC-17.1 (step 4, step 6)Reserved goods cannot be sold again, so the sellable quantity is the available quantity.System (inventory service)
GBR-IV-02SKU status: "Hết hàng" (Out of stock) when the available quantity is less than or equal to 0; "Sắp hết" (Low stock) when the minimum threshold is greater than 0 and the available quantity is less than or equal to the threshold; otherwise "Còn hàng" (In stock). A minimum threshold of 0 means the SKU is not monitored for low stock.UC-17.1 (step 4, status filter of step 3)Show at a glance which items need to be restocked.System (inventory service)
GBR-TH-01If a maximum stock threshold is given it must be greater than or equal to the minimum stock threshold.UC-17.2 (step 5, step 6)A maximum smaller than the minimum is meaningless and makes the purchase suggestion wrong.Configuration dialog and the system (inventory service)
GBR-TH-02If after the change the SKU is low on stock (minimum threshold greater than 0 and available stock less than or equal to the threshold), the system records a low-stock alert for staff review. The system does not create a purchase order and does not choose a purchase quantity by itself.UC-17.2 (step 7)Warn in time when the new threshold puts a SKU at a low level, while the purchasing decision stays with people.System (inventory service, alert handling)
GBR-TH-03The minimum threshold decides the "Sắp hết" status of the SKU (see GBR-IV-02 in UC-17.1); a minimum threshold of 0 means the SKU is not monitored. The maximum threshold is used to suggest the purchase quantity when creating a purchase order.UC-17.2 (step 8, "Step 4_Turn off the low-stock warning" flow)Thresholds are inputs for stock monitoring and purchase suggestion.System (inventory service and purchase order service)
GBR-WP-01A warranty ticket has the statuses: Chờ tiếp nhận (New), Đang xử lý (In progress), Hoàn tất (Done), Bị từ chối (Rejected), Đã hủy (Cancelled). Hoàn tất, Bị từ chối and Đã hủy are final statuses and cannot change further. For the status transition rules see GBR-WP-02 and GBR-WP-03 (UC-22.7).UC-22.6 (step 2, step 4)Shows where a ticket is in the process and which tickets still need handling.System (warranty ticket service)
GBR-WP-04A warranty card is considered expired when its expiry date is earlier than the current date. The system only warns; whether to reject or still handle the ticket is the Shop Owner's decision.UC-22.6 (step 4)The Shop Owner needs to know the card has expired but may still want to support the customer.System (warranty ticket service) and the details screen
GBR-WP-02Only a Chờ tiếp nhận ticket can be rejected; the rejection reason is required, at most 2000 characters. Once accepted, a ticket can no longer be rejected.UC-22.7 ("Step 1_Reject the ticket" flow)Rejecting makes sense only before handling starts; the reason helps the customer understand the decision.System (warranty ticket service)
GBR-WP-03Only a Đang xử lý ticket can be completed; the processing result is required, at most 4000 characters. A case found not eligible for the warranty during handling is still completed with a result "Không bảo hành vì …" and does not become Bị từ chối.UC-22.7 (step 5, "Step 4_Not covered by the warranty" flow)Every accepted ticket ends with a result so the customer gets a clear answer.System (warranty ticket service)
