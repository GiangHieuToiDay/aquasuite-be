UC-14.1 – Verify Staff-Recorded Bank Transfer

Primary ActorsAccountantSecondary ActorsNone
Created ByPham Van NamCreated Date24/11/2026
DescriptionAs an Accountant, I want to view the bank transfers that staff have recorded as pending, compare them with the money actually received in the bank account, and verify or reject each transfer, so that the collected amount of an order counts only the confirmed transfers.
Preconditions1. The user is logged in with the Accountant role.
2. There is at least one bank transfer in the "Chờ xác minh" (Pending verification) status, recorded by a staff member in the record payment use case (UC-14.2).
Normal FlowVerify a bank transfer
1. The Accountant opens the "Xác minh thanh toán chuyển khoản" page.
2. The system displays three tabs "Đang chờ", "Đã xác minh", "Đã từ chối" with their counts; the "Đang chờ" tab is open by default with the bank transfers waiting for verification, 20 rows per page, the earliest recorded transfer on top. Each row shows the transaction code, amount, reference code, note, time and the actions Chứng từ (Proof), Từ chối (Reject) and Xác minh (Verify).
3. The Accountant compares the transfer with the money actually received in the bank account (done outside the system).
4. The Accountant chooses "Xác minh" on a transfer that matches.
5. The system checks that the transfer is pending according to GBR-VF-02, that the order can receive payments and that the amount does not exceed the remaining balance according to GBR-VF-03.
6. The system records the transfer as "Đã xác minh" (Verified) with the verifier and the verification time, adds it to the order's paid amount, updates the order's payment status and allocates the receipt to invoices according to GBR-VF-04.
7. The system refreshes the list (the transfer leaves the "Đang chờ" tab) and displays “Đã xác minh <số tiền> vào đơn.”
Alternative FlowsStep 3_View the proof
1. The Accountant chooses "Chứng từ" on a transfer.
2. The system opens the bank transfer proof attached by the staff member in a new tab (or downloads it if the browser blocks the new tab).
3. The Accountant compares it and continues at step 4.

Step 3_View the order
1. The Accountant chooses the "Xem đơn →" link on a transfer.
2. The system opens the details of the order of that transfer so that the accountant can compare the amount and the payment obligation. The use case continues at step 4.

Step 4_Reject the bank transfer
1. The Accountant chooses "Từ chối" on a transfer.
2. The system displays the "Từ chối khoản chuyển khoản" dialog showing the transaction code, amount, reference code and the "Lý do từ chối" field (required, at most 1000 characters; hints: money not received in the account, wrong amount, duplicate of a received transfer).
3. The Accountant enters the reason and chooses "Từ chối".
4. The system checks that the transfer is pending (GBR-VF-02) and records it as "Đã từ chối" (Rejected) with the reason, the user who rejected it and the time; the order's paid amount and payment status do not change (GBR-VF-05).
5. The system closes the dialog, refreshes the list and displays “Đã từ chối khoản chuyển khoản.” The use case ends.

Step 4_Cancel the rejection
1. The Accountant chooses "Hủy" or closes the "Từ chối khoản chuyển khoản" dialog.
2. The system does not change the transfer. The use case ends.

Step 2_View processed transfers
1. The Accountant chooses the "Đã xác minh" or "Đã từ chối" tab.
2. The system displays the processed transfers, the most recently processed on top, with the columns "Trạng thái / Quyết định" (with the rejection reason, if any) and "Thời điểm xử lý". These transfers are view-only, with no verify or reject action. The use case ends.
Exception FlowStep 1_No permission
1. If the user is not signed in or the session has expired, the system displays “Chưa xác thực hoặc phiên đăng nhập đã hết hạn”.
2. If the user is not an Accountant (GBR-VF-01), the system does not display the list and only displays “Chỉ dành cho Kế toán”.
3. The use case ends.

Step 2_List cannot be loaded
1. If the system cannot load the list, it displays “Không tải được danh sách.” with a "Thử lại" (Retry) button.
2. The Accountant chooses "Thử lại". Return to step 2.

Step 2_No transfers
1. If the "Đang chờ" tab has no transfer, the system displays “Không có khoản chuyển khoản nào đang chờ xác minh.”
2. If another tab has no transfer, the system displays “Chưa có khoản nào ở trạng thái này.” The use case ends.

Step 5_Transfer already processed
1. If another accountant has already verified or rejected the transfer (GBR-VF-02), the system changes nothing and displays “Khoản thanh toán đã được xử lý, không thể quyết định lại”.
2. The Accountant reloads the list. Return to step 2.

Step 5_Not a pending bank transfer
1. If the item is not a pending bank transfer of the order, the system changes nothing and displays “Chỉ xử lý khoản chuyển khoản đang chờ xác minh của đơn”.
2. Return to step 2.

Step 5_Transfer or order not found
1. If the payment no longer exists, the system displays “Không tìm thấy khoản thanh toán hoặc chứng từ”.
2. If the order cannot be found, the system displays “Không tìm thấy đơn hàng”.
3. The system changes nothing. Return to step 2.

Step 5_Order cannot receive payments
1. If the order is cancelled or its total amount is not valid, the system does not verify and displays “Đơn hàng không thể nhận thanh toán”.
2. The transfer stays "Chờ xác minh". Return to step 2.

Step 5_Amount exceeds the remaining balance
1. If the amount of the transfer is greater than the order's remaining balance (GBR-VF-03), the system does not verify and displays “Số tiền vượt số còn phải trả; giữ nguyên chờ xác minh để xử lý ngoài hệ thống”.
2. The transfer stays "Chờ xác minh"; the accountant handles it outside the system (for example refunding the customer). Return to step 2.

Step 4_Rejection reason missing
1. If the reason is empty, the system displays “Lý do không được để trống.” and does not allow submission; if the data still reaches the system, the system displays “Lý do từ chối là bắt buộc”.
2. The Accountant enters the reason. Return to step 3 of the "Reject the bank transfer" flow.
Postconditions1. After step 6 the bank transfer is "Đã xác minh" with the verifier and time; the order's paid amount and payment status are updated.
2. After the "Reject the bank transfer" flow the transfer is "Đã từ chối" with the reason, the user who rejected it and the time; the order's paid amount does not change.
3. A transfer that has been verified or rejected cannot be decided again.
4. When the action is not successful, the transfer stays "Chờ xác minh" and the order data does not change.

Business Rules

GBR-VF-01Only users with the Accountant role use the bank transfer verification page; users with other roles only see the message “Chỉ dành cho Kế toán”.UC-14.1 (step 1)Separate verifying money from receiving money (Sales Staff) to control fraud.Screen (page blocked) and the system (role check)
GBR-VF-02Only a bank transfer in the "Chờ xác minh" (Pending) status can be verified or rejected. A transfer that is already verified or rejected cannot be decided again.UC-14.1 (step 5, step 4 of "Reject the bank transfer")Each transfer has exactly one final decision, so money is never added twice.System (payment verification service)
GBR-VF-03The verified amount cannot exceed the order's remaining balance. Remaining balance = order total − verified receipts − pending cash-on-delivery (COD) receipts. If it is exceeded, the transfer stays "Chờ xác minh" for handling outside the system; the system does not refund or change the order status automatically.UC-14.1 (step 5)Do not record more than the order's obligation; handling the excess needs a human decision.System (payment verification service)
GBR-VF-04Verifying a bank transfer: the transfer becomes "Đã xác minh" with the verifier and time; the amount is added to the paid amount; the order's payment status becomes Fully paid when the paid amount equals the order total (otherwise Partially paid); the system allocates the receipt to the customer's eligible electronic invoices, oldest first.UC-14.1 (step 6)Only after the accountant confirms the money has arrived does the transfer count in the debt and the order.System (payment verification service, order status update, payment allocation)
GBR-VF-05Rejecting a bank transfer: the reason is required, at most 1000 characters; the transfer becomes "Đã từ chối" with the reason, the user and the time; it is not counted in the paid amount and the order's payment status does not change.UC-14.1 ("Reject the bank transfer" flow)Keep the basis of the decision for traceability and let the staff know the transfer was not accepted.Reject dialog (reason check) and the system (re-validation, storing the decision)
